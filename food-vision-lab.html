Methods · colour science

White-point normalisation

The mean linear RGB of the white-tile ROI is compared with the tile's certified L*a*b* converted to linear sRGB; each channel is scaled by the ratio (a diagonal von Kries correction in linear RGB, which exactly removes an illuminant cast of that form).

g_c = RGB_lin,tile,certified / mean(RGB_lin,tile,measured) c ∈ {R,G,B}

ΔE00 follows Sharma, Wu & Dalal (2005) with kL = kC = kH = 1 and is verified on their 34 reference pairs in the Validation tab. ΔE classes: <1 not perceptible, 1–2 close observation, 2–3.5 perceptible, 3.5–5 clear, ≥5 different colours.

Colour-chart mode replaces the sRGB curve with a per-channel tone curve through the grey patches (ln linear vs ln code, when 3 or more greys are present) and the diagonal gains with a least-squares 3×3 matrix fitted to all reference patches. The calibration table then shows leave-one-out errors: each patch predicted by a matrix fitted without it.

The automatic mask takes the median colour of a thin image border as background, thresholds ΔE*ab from it with Otsu's method, opens (r = 2 px), fills holes and keeps the largest object.

Methods · segmentation and size statistics

Segmentation

Grey = Rec. 709 luma. Separable Gaussian (±4σ kernel, replicate borders). Otsu's threshold maximises between-class variance on a 256-bin histogram. Opening and closing use a Euclidean disk Br and are computed exactly from the distance transform (validated pixel-for-pixel against brute-force structuring elements).

The exact Euclidean distance transform uses the Felzenszwalb–Huttenlocher lower-envelope algorithm. Markers are the h-maxima of the distance map: the regional maxima of the reconstruction by dilation of (D − h) under D (Vincent's hybrid algorithm), so hills whose dip to an equal or higher hill is under h share one marker. The watershed is Meyer's priority flooding of −EDT restricted to the foreground. Without watershed, objects are labelled by two-pass union–find.

When edge-cut objects are excluded, each counted object of bounding box bw×bh gets weight W·H / ((W − bw − 1)(H − bh − 1)) in every size statistic (Miles–Lantuéjoul correction).

Methods · shrinkage and fractal dimension

Per-frame measures

Grey → Gaussian → Otsu → opening → closing → hole filling → largest component. Area is the pixel count; perimeter, circularity and Feret diameters use the same estimators as the particle analyser. The isotropy exponent z is the slope of ln(A/A0) against ln(L/L0) with L the moment-based major axis; z = 2 for isotropic shrinkage.

A/A0(t) = Y∞ + (1 − Y∞) exp(−k t) t½ = ln 2 / k fitted by nested golden-section least squares (Y∞ ∈ [0, min A/A0))

Box-counting dimension

The 8-connected inner boundary is covered with square boxes of side s (√2 steps between the smallest box and the object size ÷ 4, 8 or 16). N(s) is the minimum over four grid offsets, and D is the least-squares slope of log N against log(1/s). On validation shapes the estimator reads 0.97 for a circle, 1.28 for the Koch snowflake (1.262), and 1.981 for a filled square.

D = −d log N(s) / d log s
